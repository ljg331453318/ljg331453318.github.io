02 / PROFILE

Biography

From raw signals
to trustworthy decisions.

Jiageng Liu received the B.S. degree from Liaoning Technical University in 2008, the M.S. degree from Liaoning University of Technology in 2013, and the Ph.D. degree from Northeastern University in 2024.

Since 2008, he has worked in academic teaching and research across electrical and control engineering. His work asks a practical question: when sensors disagree, fail, or are attacked, how can a vehicle still know where it is?
